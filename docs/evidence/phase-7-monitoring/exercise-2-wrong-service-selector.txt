→ Root cause: selector app=api-gateway,tier=frontend vs pod labels app=lab-api-gateway,tier=edge.

### Fix
$ kubectl -n crm-lab patch svc lab-api-gateway --type merge -p '{"spec":{"selector":{"app":"lab-api-gateway","tier":"edge"}}}'
